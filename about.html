Faith

Faith is first for me. God has provided every opportunity for me to get here and has sustained me through every challenge along the way. My faith in Christ guides me, is the source of my peace, and gives me purpose.

Romans 12:1-2 MSG: "So here's what I want you to do, God helping you: Take your everyday, ordinary life-your sleeping, eating. Going-to-work and walking-around life can be placed before God as an offering. Embracing what God does for you is the best thing you can do for him. Do not become so adjusted to your culture that you fit into it without thinking. Instead, fix your attention on God. You will be changed from the inside out. Readily recognize what he wants from you, and quickly respond to it. Unlike the culture around you, always dragging you down. God brings the best out of you and develops well-formed maturity in you."

Family

On July 5th 1998, my parents left everything they knew in Colombia in search of greater opportunities in the United States. They are my greatest role models and have shown me what it means to make something out of nothing. Their work ethic, generosity, and unconditional love have shaped me as a person greatly.

In 2019, I met Zane Nolte, the coolest, kindest, most ambitious person out there (imo)! Originally from South Africa, Zane and his family moved to Germany when he was 5 and then the U.S. in 2018 as his dad's job required. I'm blessed with incredible in-laws and an awesome brother in law who brings my brother collection to 4 in total. Yes, I am the only daughter.

Zane is my biggest cheerleader and someone who inspires me every day. After 7 years together (2 years married), I'm grateful for all we've accomplished and all the advenutres to come.

Friends

I genuinely believe the people around you shape who you become. Friendships and community aren't just nice to have for me, they're essential. I'm someone who invests deeply in relationships, shows up for the people I love, and finds so much joy in doing life with people rather than just alongside them.

These are a few of the people Zane and I do life with the most. Jirielle, Anna, and Darien are shoulders to cry on, people to belly laugh with, always down for a side quest (like auditioning for a musical together) and the kind of friends who push you to become the best version of yourself. Life is genuinely just better in community. SHOUTOUT FRIENDSHIP!
